# Privacy

Bulle Block collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

- The block lists are built into the extension. It never downloads lists, and it never reports which pages you visit or what it blocked.
- Your settings (which lists are on, the sites you paused) are kept in your browser's extension storage. If browser sync is on, the browser syncs them between your own devices.
- It needs access to every site to hide ad spaces in pages and to filter sponsored posts on Facebook. That happens inside your browser.
